### What is this?

These are my personal privilege escalation notes — built over years of playing CTF machines on HackTheBox, TryHackMe and FlagYard.

Not copied from a course. Every technique here is something I actually used on a real machine and wrote down so I wouldn't forget it.

### Who is this for?

- CTF players who want a quick reference during a machine
- Beginners learning Linux privilege escalation
- Anyone preparing for certifications like eCPPT, OSCP, or CRTP
